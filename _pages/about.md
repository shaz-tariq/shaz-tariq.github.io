I am Shaz, a DPhil student based in the Gene Medicine Group, Radcliffe Department of Medicine, University of Oxford. 

Due to the stratospheric rise of CRISPR-Cas based genome engineering technologies, we have multiple strategies for treating every type of genetic disease. Realising this potential, however, requires safe and efficient ways of delivering these editors. My work is aiming to address this by developing and engineering novel types of virus-like particles for the targeted devilry of genome editors. 

I am particularly interested in finding ways to package and deliver larger, more complex cargo such as prime editors and emerging gene insertion technologies (eePASSIGE, Prime Assembly). 

You can find my CV, publications, and research interests using the links above.
